What is the Foum Zguid desert?

“The Foum Zguid desert” is shorthand for everything south of town, inside Iriki National Park (also spelt Iriqui) and beyond. It is not one landscape but several, one after another:

  • The Lake Iriki plain: an old lake that is almost always bone dry, with salty, cracked ground. After exceptional rain it can hold shallow water for a while. Read our Lake Iriki guide.
  • Erg Lmhazil (El Mhazil): a smaller, quieter dune field, good for walking and for camping well away from anyone else. More on Erg Lmhazil.
  • Erg Chigaga (Chegaga): Morocco’s largest sand sea, around 35–40 km long and roughly 15 km wide, with most dunes in the 60–80 m range and a few higher. See our Erg Chigaga guide.
  • Oases and villages: Zaouiat Sidi Abdenabi below the Jebel Bani, Laarjimia, El Grisim, and Tissint towards Tata.

In between, the track runs through acacia steppe, patches of tamarisk and flat-topped hills. There is no tarmac at all, which is why every one of our Foum Zguid desert tours uses a 4×4 and a driver who knows the tracks.

Which trip suits you: a day, one night, two nights or a trek?

It mostly comes down to how much time you have and how you like to travel.

  • Just passing through (one day): Lake Iriki with lunch at Titanic Iriki (from €90) is a relaxed day out. Erg Chigaga in a day (from €125) is doable, but it is a long one, with a lot of driving for a short time on the sand.
  • The classic (one night): this is where most people get the best value for their time. You see sunset and sunrise, a proper night sky, and eat dinner in camp. Pick Erg Chigaga (from €150) for the big dunes, Erg El Mhazil (from €140) for solitude, or the two-day Foum Zguid desert trip with fossils and an oasis (from €130).
  • Slow travel (two nights): the Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga loop (from €270) adds a night at Titanic Iriki and a nomad school; the desert hike with nomad school visit (from €260) adds around four hours’ walking.
  • On foot (trek): camels carry the kit while you walk. Four days in Iriki National Park (from €310) or six days from El Grisim oasis to Erg Chigaga (from €650). Our Sahara trekking guide explains how the days work.

Travelling with children, managing a health condition or short of time? Tell us when you enquire and we will adjust the pace, the stops and the hours on the track.

Sample itinerary: the Foum Zguid desert in 2 days / 1 night

To show how a trip actually runs, here is the Foum Zguid desert 2 days / 1 night trip (from €130). Times are a guide and shift with the seasons.

Day 1: Foum Zguid to Erg Lamhazil

  • Midday: pick-up by 4×4 in Foum Zguid.
  • Across the old bed of Lake Iriki, with a stop at a site of marine fossils.
  • Around 4 pm: arrive in the dunes and settle into camp.
  • Sunset on foot, or by camel if you would like (optional).
  • Dinner, music and a night in a tent.

Day 2: village, nomad family and oasis

  • Around 6 am: sunrise over the dunes, then breakfast in camp.
  • Through Zaouit Sidi Abdennabi, a traditional village below the Jebel Bani.
  • A visit to a nomad family.
  • Lunch at Laajamiya oasis.
  • Around 5 pm: back in Foum Zguid.

Want the tallest dunes instead? The Erg Chigaga overnight desert tour (from €150) follows the same rhythm: across Lake Iriki, a camel ride, sunset and a night in a nomad tent, then back the next morning.

What the itineraries include

Each trip page sets out its own programme, but as a rule our itineraries cover:

  • private 4×4 transport from Foum Zguid with a local guide;
  • on overnight trips, dinner, a night in camp (nomad tent or under the stars) and breakfast;

A camel ride is built into some programmes (the two-day Erg Chigaga trip and the three-day loop) and offered as an extra on others (the Erg Chigaga day trip, Erg El Mhazil). We confirm in writing exactly what your price covers before you book, so do ask.

Best time for a Foum Zguid desert tour

The season runs roughly from October to April. What to expect through the year:

MonthsWhat to expect
October – NovemberA good time to go: the summer heat has eased and nights are comfortable.
December – FebruaryPleasant days, but desert nights can drop close to freezing. Bring proper warm layers for camp.
March – AprilAnother good window, though sandstorms can blow up; your guide will adapt the plan if they do.
May – SeptemberVery hot, often above 40 °C by day. Start early, keep trips short and drink plenty.

What to pack for the desert

  • Sun protection: a hat or headscarf, sunglasses and high-factor sun cream.
  • Warm layers for the evening and night, even in spring: a fleece, a windproof jacket and a hat in winter.
  • Closed shoes for stony ground, plus sandals for the sand if you like.
  • A head torch and a power bank, as you cannot count on a socket in camp.
  • Cash in dirhams, withdrawn before you reach Foum Zguid.

For treks, ask us for the full kit list: depending on the season you will want a sleeping bag, walking boots and a daypack.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Foum Zguid desert tour cost?

Our “from” prices per person start at €90 for a day trip (Lake Iriki and Titanic Iriki, or Laarjimia oasis), €150 for a night at Erg Chigaga, €270 for three days and €650 for a six-day trek. The final price depends on your dates and group size, and we confirm it with you before you book.

How long is the drive from Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga?

Allow roughly 2.5 to 3 hours on the 4×4 track, depending on conditions and how often you stop. The route crosses Iriki National Park and the dry lake bed, so the drive is part of the day. That is why we suggest at least one night: on a day trip your time in the dunes is short.

Erg Chigaga or Erg Lmhazil: which should I choose?

Choose Erg Chigaga, Morocco’s largest sand sea with most dunes around 60–80 m, if you want the big, classic Sahara view. Erg Lmhazil (El Mhazil) is smaller and quieter, and suits walking and a night away from other camps. Both are reached from Foum Zguid across Lake Iriki.

Is Erg Chigaga better than Merzouga?

It depends what you are after. Merzouga (Erg Chebbi), near Erfoud, is the most visited desert in Morocco and the easiest to reach, so it is busier. Erg Chigaga is more remote: you can only get there by track, from M’Hamid in the east or Foum Zguid in the west, and the camps are more spread out.

Can I drive into the Foum Zguid desert myself?

We would not recommend it. Beyond Foum Zguid there is no tarmac, and the tracks to Lake Iriki, Erg Lmhazil and Erg Chigaga are unmarked, mixing soft sand and stony ground. Getting stuck or lost is a real risk without local knowledge. All our tours use a private 4×4 with a guide who knows the terrain.

When is the best time to visit the Foum Zguid desert?

From October to April. Summer days often exceed 40 °C. Winter days are pleasant, but nights in camp can approach freezing, so pack warm clothes. In spring, sandstorms can occur, in which case your guide will adjust the route and timings. For treks, aim for the cooler months.

Are the tours private or shared?

All our tours are private: the 4×4 and guide are just for you, whether you are a couple, a family or a group of friends. That means the stops, pace and departure time can be adapted to you. We confirm the programme and price with you on WhatsApp before you book.
