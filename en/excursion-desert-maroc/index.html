What a day looks like: the 4×4 day trip to Erg Chigaga

The Erg Chigaga day trip from Foum Zguid (from €125) shows how a short desert excursion runs:

  1. Morning: you set off by 4×4 along the old Paris–Dakar track through Iriki National Park, stopping at Zaouait Sidi Abdenabi for lunch in a traditional mud-brick house.
  2. Afternoon: the track winds through tamarisk to the dunes. There is welcome tea at the camp and time to climb a dune; an hour-long camel ride can be added as an option.
  3. Return: you cross the vast dry bed of Lake Iriki, with photo stops on the plain, before arriving back in Foum Zguid.

Allow about 2.5 to 3 hours of track each way, depending on conditions and stops. It is a long day, so if you want sunset on the dunes, staying the night is more comfortable. Our guide to Erg Chigaga from Foum Zguid has more detail.

Overnight desert trips in Morocco: four ways to spend the night

A night in the desert changes everything: evening light, dinner by the fire, a sky full of stars and sunrise over the sand. Our four two-day, one-night trips differ mainly in where you sleep.

  • Erg Chigaga camp (from €150): 4×4 across the Iriki plain, a camel ride, sunset, dinner round the fire, a night in a nomad tent and the drive back after breakfast.
  • Foum Zguid desert in 2 days (from €130): pick-up at midday, a marine fossil site, the Erg Lamhazil dunes at about 4 pm, music and a night under canvas; next day Zaouit Sidi Abdennabi, a nomad family, lunch at Laajamiya oasis and back around 5 pm.
  • Erg El Mhazil camp (from €140): a quieter alternative to the big dunes, with a traditional dinner and an evening round the fire; camel ride optional.
  • Titanic Iriki and camp (from €140): lunch and dinner at Titanic Iriki, a night under the stars or in a mud-brick room, back around midday.

To see how the dune fields differ, read our pages on Erg Lmhazil and Lake Iriki.

Three days: more than a there-and-back

With three days, the desert stops being a destination and becomes somewhere you live for a while.

  • Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga adventure (from €270): an afternoon start on about 60 km of track, a fossil site and a night at Titanic Iriki; on day two a nomad school visit, then camp at Erg Chigaga; on day three a sunrise walk in the dunes and the drive back across Lake Iriki.
  • Desert hike and nomad school visit (from €260): camp at Erg El Mhazil, about four hours’ walking on day two with a camel caravan carrying the gear, a night at Erg El Ghoule, then a two-hour walk to a nomad school, visited when the families are able to welcome guests.

Private desert tour or shared group?

All our excursions are private departures: the vehicle and guide are just for you, your family or your friends. That makes a real difference:

  • Pace: you can ask for longer on a dune or an extra photo stop.
  • Timing: start times can be discussed around your arrival in Foum Zguid; ask when you book.
  • Conversation: your guide can answer your questions about the desert, nomad life and Iriki park.

Shared tours, common from the big cities, put strangers together on a fixed programme. They can be cheaper but leave less room to adapt. Whatever you choose, compare the price against what is included and how many people will be in the vehicle.

Morocco desert excursions by 4×4: tracks and practical tips

Beyond Foum Zguid the tarmac gives way to the tracks of Iriki (Iriqui) National Park. A 4×4 is not a gimmick here: the Lake Iriki plain, sandy stretches and the approach to the dunes call for a suitable vehicle and a driver who knows the ground.

  • After heavy rain, Lake Iriki can hold water for a while, and the route is adjusted.
  • Keep water, a hat and sunglasses within reach in the vehicle.
  • The track is bumpy, so mention any back problems or travel sickness.
  • Summer days are often above 40 °C; the best season runs from October to April.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best one-day desert tour in Morocco from Foum Zguid?

The Erg Chigaga day trip (from €125) takes you to Morocco’s largest erg across Iriki National Park. If you would rather spend less time on the track, the Lake Iriki and Titanic Iriki day trip (from €90) or the Laarjimia oasis excursion (from €90) are gentler options.

How much is an overnight desert trip from Foum Zguid?

Our two-day, one-night trips start from €130 per person for the Foum Zguid desert programme (fossils, Erg Lamhazil, oasis), from €140 for the Erg El Mhazil camp or the night at Titanic Iriki, and from €150 for a camp at Erg Chigaga. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

How long is the drive from Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga?

Allow about 2.5 to 3 hours of 4×4 track, depending on conditions and stops. The route crosses Iriki National Park and the Lake Iriki plain. On a day trip you do this each way, which is why many travellers prefer to spend a night at camp and enjoy the dunes.

Are your desert tours private?

Yes. Every excursion is a private departure, so the vehicle and guide are reserved for your group, family or friends. The pace can adapt to you within the programme. Tell us how many people are travelling when you enquire and you will receive a confirmed price.

Do I need a 4×4 for a desert trip?

Yes, for Erg Chigaga, Erg El Mhazil and Lake Iriki. The tracks of Iriki National Park cross open plains and sandy sections that call for a suitable vehicle and a driver who knows the terrain. All our excursions run by 4×4 with the guide, starting from Foum Zguid.

What are your payment and cancellation terms?

We do not set them out on this page. They are sent to you in writing on WhatsApp, together with the programme and price, before you book. Take time to read them and ask any questions before confirming. Our WhatsApp number is +212 649 598 615.

Is a desert tour suitable for children?

Yes, if you choose a short format. A Lake Iriki day trip or a night at Erg El Mhazil involves far less driving than starting from a big city. Mention your children’s ages when you enquire, and the guide will adjust the breaks and advise on the camel ride.
