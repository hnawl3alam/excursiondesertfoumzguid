Why go to Erg Chigaga from Foum Zguid?

Foum Zguid is the western gateway to Erg Chigaga: a small oasis town below the Jebel Bani on the N12 between Tata and Zagora, about 150 km from Ouarzazate via Tazenakht. It is also where the tarmac ends. From here on it is desert track all the way to the dunes.

The reason to start here is the journey itself. The track runs through Iriki National Park, created in 1994 and covering about 123,000 ha, then across the Lake Iriki plain, a vast lakebed that is dry most of the time. That turns the trip into a full day in the desert rather than a transfer to a camp. The other main approach is from M’Hamid in the east; for the dunes themselves, the camps and the seasons, see our Erg Chigaga guide.

Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga: the track, stage by stage

The stops vary with the itinerary, but the drive usually follows this pattern:

  1. Leaving Foum Zguid by 4×4. The day trip follows the old Paris–Dakar rally track, between hills and flat-topped mesas.
  2. Iriki National Park: wide stony plains, acacias and dry plateaus.
  3. Zaouiat Sidi Abdenabi: on the day trip, a lunch stop in a traditional mud-brick house.
  4. The Lake Iriki plain: flat, cracked and in places salty, and made for photo stops. On the 3-day trip a fossil site and the Titanic Iriki, a guesthouse known for its ship-like décor, are added along the way.
  5. Tamarisk scrub signals that the dunes are close, shortly before you reach camp.

Without long breaks, allow about 2.5–3 hours on the track. With lunch and photo stops, the outward leg fills much of the day, and that is the point: the crossing is part of the trip. The track is bumpy in places, so mention it in advance if you suffer from motion sickness.

Erg Chigaga day trip from Foum Zguid

The Erg Chigaga day trip from Foum Zguid, from €125 per person, runs like this:

  • Morning: leave Foum Zguid by 4×4 on the old Paris–Dakar track through Iriki National Park.
  • Midday: stop at Zaouiat Sidi Abdenabi for lunch in a traditional mud-brick house.
  • Afternoon: through the tamarisks to the dunes, welcome tea at camp and free time to climb a dune. A camel ride of about an hour can be added on request.
  • Return: across the huge dry bed of Lake Iriki, with photo stops, back to Foum Zguid.

It is a long day on the track. It suits you if you only have one day or would rather not sleep in the desert, but you will miss sunset and sunrise on the dunes.

Erg Chegaga tour from Foum Zguid: 2 days / 1 night

The Erg Chigaga desert adventure, 2 days / 1 night, from €150 per person, is the best balance for most people:

  • Day 1: 4×4 from Foum Zguid, across the dry Lake Iriki plain to the dunes. Camel ride, sunset, dinner round the fire and a night in a nomad tent.
  • Day 2: sunrise and breakfast at camp, then back by 4×4 across the Iriki plain and past the mesas to Foum Zguid.

The driving is the same as on the day trip, but split over two days, so you get the evening and the early morning in the dunes, the best hours for light and quiet.

3 days / 2 nights: fossils, Titanic Iriki and a nomad school

The Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga adventure over 3 days, from €270, adds a night at the Titanic Iriki:

  • Day 1: afternoon departure from Foum Zguid on about 60 km of track, a stop at a fossil site, sunset tea, dinner and the night at the Titanic Iriki.
  • Day 2: on through arid country, a visit to a nomad school, then Erg Chigaga: camel ride, dinner, music and a night at camp.
  • Day 3: sunrise, a walk in the dunes, and back across Lake Iriki with stops on the cracked plains.

Practical tips for the trip

  • Book ahead, especially in holiday periods. We confirm the programme and price before you book, on WhatsApp (+212 649 598 615) or via the contact page.
  • Meeting point: agreed with you in Foum Zguid when you book.
  • Pack light: a small overnight bag is all you need.
  • Protect your camera from dust and sand.
  • Bring warm layers for the evening from October to April, and light, covering clothes for the day.
  • After rain, Lake Iriki can hold some water and the route may change: trust the driver’s call.
  • Never drive into the dunes alone in your own vehicle.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to drive from Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga?

Allow about 2.5–3 hours by 4×4 between Foum Zguid and the dunes of Erg Chigaga, depending on the state of the track. With the lunch and photo stops built into our itineraries, the outward leg takes up a good part of the day. The return crosses the Lake Iriki plain and takes a similar time.

How much is an Erg Chigaga tour from Foum Zguid?

Our prices start from €125 per person for the day trip, €150 for the 2-day / 1-night desert camp trip and €270 for 3 days / 2 nights including a night at the Titanic Iriki. All trips are private; the exact price depends on the programme and is confirmed with you before you book.

Can I drive to Erg Chigaga from Foum Zguid in my own car?

Not in an ordinary car: no tarmac road reaches the dunes. Even with a 4×4, going alone is strongly discouraged, because vehicles bog down easily in soft sand and tracks multiply across the Iriki plain. Leave your car in Foum Zguid and make the crossing with a local driver who knows current track conditions.

Is a day trip enough, or should I stay overnight?

A day trip is enough to see the dunes, but it means roughly five to six hours on the track in total, and you leave before sunset. A night at camp gives you the evening and the early morning, the finest hours, and spreads the driving over two days. If you can spare the time, stay the night.

Is the camel ride included?

On the 2-day / 1-night trip and on the 3-day / 2-night trip, a camel ride is part of the programme. On the day trip, a ride of about an hour can be added as an option. Mention it when you book so that it is written into the confirmed programme and there are no surprises on the day.

Can I start in Zagora or Ouarzazate and come back via Foum Zguid?

Yes. Our 2-day / 1-night circuits from Zagora (from €235) and Ouarzazate (from €300) reach Erg Chigaga via the Drâa Valley and M’Hamid, then return across Lake Iriki and through Foum Zguid. That way you see both approaches to the desert without driving the same track twice.

What happens if it has rained on Lake Iriki?

After exceptional rain, Lake Iriki can briefly hold a thin layer of water and some tracks turn muddy. The driver will then take another line or adjust the programme. Get in touch a few days before you travel and we will tell you how the track is and confirm the route with you.
