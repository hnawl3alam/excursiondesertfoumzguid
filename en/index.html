Why leave from Foum Zguid

The desert begins before the dunes.

Foum Zguid is a quiet southern gateway to the Sahara. From here, the track crosses palm-fringed foothills, the mineral expanse of Lake Iriki and, farther south, the dunes of Erg Chigaga and Erg Lmhazil.

A direct gateway

Reach Lake Iriki, Erg Chigaga and Erg Lmhazil without turning the journey into a race.

A local departure

Meet your guide in Foum Zguid and confirm the route according to your dates and the conditions.

A private rhythm

Photo stops, family travel, trekking or bivouac: the itinerary adapts to how you want to experience the desert.

Before the first track.

Answers to the questions we are asked most often on WhatsApp.

Which desert can I visit from Foum Zguid?

The main routes lead to Lake Iriki, Erg Chigaga and Erg Lmhazil. The best choice depends on your available time and whether you prefer 4×4, trekking or a bivouac.

How many days should I plan?

A first experience is possible in one day. For sunset and a night in camp, allow two days. Multi-day treks and circuits offer a more complete immersion.

Are departures private?

The published excursions are planned around your dates and group. Confirm the exact format, vehicle and services directly before booking.

What should I bring?

Bring sun protection, water, closed shoes and a warm layer for evenings. Specific trekking or camp equipment is confirmed according to the chosen journey.

How do I book?

Send your dates, number of travellers and departure city on WhatsApp. A local guide will confirm availability and practical details.
