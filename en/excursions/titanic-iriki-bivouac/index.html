Day by day

1

Day 1 — Iriki and Titanic

Early departure by 4×4 through Iriki National Park, exploring the salt plain, then lunch at Titanic Iriki. The afternoon is free or can include an optional guided walk. Moroccan dinner and a night under the stars or in a traditional rammed-earth room.

2

Day 2 — Sunrise and return

Traditional breakfast, optional morning stroll, then return towards Foum Zguid in the late morning, with stops to take in the landscape. Arrival expected around midday.
