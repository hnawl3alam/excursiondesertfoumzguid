Day by day

1

Day 1 — Ouarzazate to Erg Chigaga

Pick-up by 4×4 from your hotel at 8:00. The route runs along the Drâa Valley, with stops in Zagora and Tamegroute, then lunch in M’Hamid. After the Sacred Oasis, arrival at the dunes is followed by sunset, dinner by the fire and a night at the desert camp.

2

Day 2 — Erg Chigaga to Ouarzazate

Sunrise and breakfast. The return by 4×4 passes through Lake Iriki, Foum Zguid and the Zguid Valley, before lunch in Tazenakht and arrival in Ouarzazate in the late afternoon.
