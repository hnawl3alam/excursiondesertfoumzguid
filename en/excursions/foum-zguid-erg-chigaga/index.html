Day by day

1

Day 1 — Fossils and Titanic Iriki

Afternoon departure from Foum Zguid by 4×4 along about 60 km of track. The programme includes a stop at a fossil site, then arrival at Titanic Iriki for sunset tea, dinner and the night.

2

Day 2 — Nomad school and Erg Chigaga

After breakfast, the route crosses dunes and arid landscapes. A visit to a nomad school comes before arrival at Erg Chigaga, with a camel ride, dinner, a music evening and a night at the desert camp.

3

Day 3 — Erg Chigaga and return

Sunrise, time to walk in the dunes, then return by 4×4 across Lake Iriki. Stops are planned on the cracked plains before arrival in Foum Zguid.
