Grand desert tour from Foum Zguid, 4 days

A four-day loop from Foum Zguid with three nights at camp: Lake Iriki, a full day at Erg Chigaga, the quiet dunes of Erg El Mhazil and the rock carvings of the Jebel Bani.

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Foum Zguid to Erg Chigaga

Departure after lunch. Across Lake Iriki and its coloured rocks to reach the dunes for sunset. Camel ride, dinner and a first night at camp.

2

Day 2 — A day at Erg Chigaga

A dune walk at sunrise, then a free day: sandboarding, rest in the tent, a visit to a nomad family if they are around. Second night at the same camp.

3

Day 3 — Erg Chigaga to Erg El Mhazil

Tracks heading west with a stop at an oasis, then Erg El Mhazil by late afternoon: smaller dunes and very few camps. Third night at camp.

4

Day 4 — Rock carvings and return

Back along the Jebel Bani with a stop at the rock carvings, lunch in the village and the end of the tour in Foum Zguid in the early afternoon.

Questions

Who is this tour for?

For travellers who want to see the whole Foum Zguid desert without rushing: two ergs, Lake Iriki and short track sections. It is our most complete loop from Foum Zguid.

Do I need to be fit?

No. You travel by 4×4 and the dune walks are short and optional. The pace is set for your group.
