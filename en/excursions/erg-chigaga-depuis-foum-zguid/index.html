Day by day

1

Morning — Departure from Foum Zguid

You set off by 4×4 along the old Paris-Dakar track, through the landscapes of Iriki National Park. A stop at Zaouait Sidi Abdenabi allows for lunch in a traditional rammed-earth house.

2

Afternoon — Towards Erg Chigaga

The track winds through tamarisk trees before reaching the dunes. A welcome tea is served at the camp, followed by time to climb a dune. A one-hour camel ride can be requested as an option.

3

Return — Lake Iriki

The return journey crosses the vast dry lake of Iriki, with photo stops on the plain before arriving in Foum Zguid.
