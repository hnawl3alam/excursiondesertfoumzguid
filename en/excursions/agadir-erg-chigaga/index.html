Agadir to Erg Chigaga via Taroudant

Three days from Agadir to the big dunes of Erg Chigaga across the Anti-Atlas: Taroudant, the saffron of Taliouine, a night in Foum Zguid and a night at a desert camp.

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Agadir to Foum Zguid

Departure around 8:00 from your accommodation in Agadir, Taghazout or Tamraght. A stop at the walls of Taroudant, lunch on the way and a saffron stop in Taliouine, then arrival in Foum Zguid in the late afternoon. Night in a guesthouse.

2

Day 2 — Lake Iriki and Erg Chigaga

A full day on the 4×4 track: across the dry bed of Lake Iriki, past coloured rocks and a fossil plateau, nomad-style lunch, then the dunes before sunset. Camel ride, dinner and a night at the desert camp.

3

Day 3 — Erg Chigaga to Agadir

Sunrise on the ridges and breakfast at camp. Out of the desert and back on the road, arriving at your hotel in the early evening.
