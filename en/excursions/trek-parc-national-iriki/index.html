Trek in Iriki National Park

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Foum Zguid to Erg El Mhazil

Departure at 14:00 by 4×4, crossing the national park and Lake Iriki. Desert camp in the dunes, a climb for sunset, dinner by the fire and a night in a tent or under the stars.

2

Day 2 — Walking in the dunes

After a Berber breakfast, a gentle trek with a camel caravan and a new camp in the dunes.

3

Day 3 — Oasis and village

Trek to a nomad oasis, tea shared with the nomads, then arrival in Zouat Sidi Abdnabi. Lunch and a night in an earthen guesthouse.

4

Day 4 — Return to Foum Zguid

Breakfast, then return by 4×4 via Lake Iriki, with arrival expected in the late morning.
