Trek in the heart of the dunes

Five days on foot in the dunes, between Erg El Mhazil, Erg El Ghoule and the El Grisim oasis.

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Arrival at Erg El Mhazil

Departure from Marrakech via Tizi N’Tichka, or from Agadir at the foot of the Atlas. After Tazenakht, Foum Zguid and the Iriki plains, camp is set up at Erg El Mhazil.

2

Day 2 — To Erg Elhaj Ahmed

Breakfast, then a walk with a camel caravan carrying the equipment. Camp is pitched in the dunes.

3

Day 3 — To Erg El Ghoule

Another day of trekking, observing the desert, followed by an evening around the fire.

4

Day 4 — El Grisim oasis

Walk to the oasis, with an introduction to sand bread and the tea ritual, dinner and a night beneath the palms.

5

Day 5 — Return

Return by 4×4 to Foum Zguid via the Iriki plain, a stop in Tazenakht, then transfer to Marrakech or Agadir.
