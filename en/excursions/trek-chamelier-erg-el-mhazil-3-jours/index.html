Camel trek to Erg El Mhazil, 3 days

Three days on foot with a camel caravan from the palm grove of Foum Zguid, along the Jebel Bani to the dunes of Erg El Mhazil and the edge of Lake Iriki.

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Foum Zguid to the foot of the Jebel Bani

The camels are loaded and you set off on foot from the palm grove in the late morning. About four hours of walking south, lunch in the shade, first camp at the foot of the Jebel Bani and dinner by the fire.

2

Day 2 — Towards the Erg El Mhazil dunes

Stones give way to sand. Five to six hours of walking with breaks and a long rest in the heat of the day; camp among the dunes.

3

Day 3 — Edge of Lake Iriki and return

An early start to walk in the cool to the edge of Lake Iriki. Lunch, then back to Foum Zguid by 4×4, about 2 hours.

Questions

Do I have to ride the camel all day?

No. You alternate: most people walk beside the caravan much of the time and ride when they feel like it, on long flat stretches or at the end of the day.

How fit do I need to be?

Regular walking fitness is enough: four to six hours a day, with breaks and a rest in the heat of the day. The camel is there when you need a break.
