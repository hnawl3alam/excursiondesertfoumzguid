Agadir to Chigaga desert trekking

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Agadir to Erg El Mhazil

Early departure by 4×4 from Agadir via Taroudant and Taliouine, entering Iriki National Park, then a desert camp at Erg El Mhazil. The programme includes welcome tea, dinner by the fire and a night in a tent or under the stars.

2

Day 2 — Erg El Mhazil to Erg Elhaj Ahmed

Morning trek with a camel caravan, then setting up camp in the heart of the dunes.

3

Day 3 — To Erg El Ghoule

Walking in the dunes, observing Saharan wildlife and plants, followed by an evening of stories, music and nomadic traditions.

4

Day 4 — El Grisim oasis

Walk to the oasis, learning about the role of oases, making sand bread and the tea ritual; night beneath the palms.

5

Day 5 — Tamarisk dunes

Trek through a semi-arid landscape of tamarisk trees, then camp at the foot of the dunes.

6

Day 6 — Arrival at Erg Chigaga

Final walk to Erg Chigaga, climbing the great dune at sunset and a night at the camp.
