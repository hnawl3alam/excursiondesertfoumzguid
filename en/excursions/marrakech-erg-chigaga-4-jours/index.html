Marrakech to Erg Chigaga via Foum Zguid, 4 days

Four days from Marrakech, including a full day at Erg Chigaga with no driving: a night in Foum Zguid, two nights at a desert camp and the return through the Drâa valley.

Day by day

1

Day 1 — Marrakech to Foum Zguid

Departure around 8:00 from your riad, over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, a visit to Aït Ben Haddou and lunch in Tazenakht. Arrival in Foum Zguid in the late afternoon, night in a guesthouse.

2

Day 2 — Lake Iriki and Erg Chigaga

On the 4×4 track in the morning: Lake Iriki, coloured rocks and a fossil plateau. Nomad-style lunch, the dunes by late afternoon, a camel ride, dinner by the fire and a night at camp.

3

Day 3 — A full day at Erg Chigaga

No driving today: a dune walk at sunrise, sandboarding, rest in the shade and tea with a nomad family if the chance arises. Second night at camp.

4

Day 4 — Erg Chigaga to Marrakech

Sunrise in the erg, out of the desert and back to Marrakech through the Drâa valley and Ouarzazate. Arrival in the early evening.

Questions

How is it different from the 3-day circuit?

The fourth day adds a full day in the dunes and a second night at camp. The 3-day circuit (from €450) leaves only one evening and one morning at Erg Chigaga.

When is the best season?

The circuit runs all year. October to April is milder; in July and August the dune night may be replaced by a night at the oasis.
