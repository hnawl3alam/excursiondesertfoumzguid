What and where is Erg El Mhazil?

Erg El Mhazil is a field of sand dunes in the desert around Foum Zguid, southern Morocco. The name turns up in several spellings – Erg El Mhazil, Erg Lmhazil, Erg Elmhazil, Erg Lamhazil – but they all mean the same place.

You reach it by 4×4 from Foum Zguid, through Iriki National Park and across the Lake Iriki plain. It is much smaller than Erg Chigaga, the great dune sea of the south, and sees far fewer visitors. There is little written about it, and the people who come are mostly after peace and quiet, a silent evening and dunes to walk without meeting anyone.

The El Mhazil dunes are also a starting point for walking. Several of our treks spend their first night here before heading off on foot, with a camel caravan, to Erg Elhaj Ahmed, Erg El Ghoule, the El Grisim oasis and, on the longest route, Erg Chigaga.

How to get to Erg El Mhazil from Foum Zguid

No tarmac road reaches the dunes, so you travel by 4×4 with a local driver. From Foum Zguid the track crosses the salty, cracked flats of Lake Iriki to the camp among the dunes; the way back runs over dry plateaus.

Driving time depends on the route and the stops. As a guide, these are the timings in our itineraries:

  • Foum Zguid desert, 2 days / 1 night: 12 noon pick-up, across the old bed of Lake Iriki, a stop at a marine fossil site, and arrival at the dunes around 4 pm.
  • Iriki National Park trek: 2 pm departure by 4×4, camp in the dunes that evening.
  • Desert hike and nomad school: early-afternoon departure through Iriki National Park.

From Marrakech (over the Tizi n’Tichka pass) or Agadir, our treks include the transfer via Tazenakht and Foum Zguid. For every trip from town, see Foum Zguid desert trips and prices.

A night at an Erg El Mhazil desert camp

The Erg El Mhazil desert adventure, 2 days / 1 night, from €140 per person, runs like this:

  • Day 1: 4×4 from Foum Zguid across the salty, cracked flats of Lake Iriki to camp among the dunes. Sunset, an optional camel ride, a traditional dinner and an evening round the fire.
  • Day 2: sunrise and breakfast, then back by 4×4 over the dry plateaus to Foum Zguid.

The Foum Zguid desert, 2 days / 1 night variant, from €130, adds a marine fossil site on the way out. Next day, after sunrise at around 6 am, it passes through the village of Zaouiat Sidi Abdenabi, visits a nomad family and stops for lunch at the Laajamiya oasis, getting back to Foum Zguid at around 5 pm.

Our Erg El Mhazil trips do not offer a luxury camp: you sleep in a tent, or under the stars on some treks. Ask what the bedding includes before you book, and bring warm clothes in winter.

Trekking from Erg El Mhazil

The terrain is ideal for walking, and several of our Sahara treks from Foum Zguid begin here. A camel caravan carries the kit; you walk with a daypack.

  • Desert hike and nomad school, 3 days: a night at Erg El Mhazil, about four hours’ walk to Erg El Ghoule, then two hours on the last day to a nomad school, visited when the community is able to receive guests.
  • Iriki National Park trek, 4 days: dunes, an easy-paced walk, a nomad oasis and a night in an earth-built guesthouse at Zaouiat Sidi Abdenabi.
  • Trek in the heart of the dunes, 5 days: from Erg El Mhazil to Erg Elhaj Ahmed, Erg El Ghoule and the El Grisim oasis, with sand-baked bread and the tea ritual.
  • Agadir to Chigaga desert trekking, 7 days: the full crossing from Erg El Mhazil to Erg Chigaga by way of the tamarisk dunes.

The pace suits a reasonably fit walker: the stages given in our itineraries range from about two to four hours’ walking. Ask for the day-by-day detail before you choose.

When to go and what to bring

The best months are roughly October to April. Summer is very hot, often above 40 °C by day, which makes walking hard work; in winter, desert nights can drop close to freezing. Sandstorms can occur in spring. After exceptional rain, Lake Iriki can briefly hold water and the route may change.

  • A scarf or shesh, sunglasses, sun cream and a hat.
  • A fleece or light down jacket for the evening; a warm hat in winter.
  • Comfortable, lightweight walking shoes for treks.
  • A head torch, a water bottle and any medication.
  • A bag to carry your rubbish out.

Safety and respect

Smaller than Chigaga it may be, but Erg El Mhazil is still remote desert. Never head into it alone by car: vehicles bog down quickly and tracks blur together on the Iriki plain. Travel with a local driver-guide, carry spare water and don’t count on mobile signal.

When you meet nomad families or visit the school, go at their pace: ask before taking photos and follow your guide’s lead.

Frequently asked questions

Is it Erg El Mhazil, Erg Lmhazil or Erg Elmhazil?

All three are right and refer to the same dune field in the Foum Zguid desert. The name is transliterated in different ways, which is why you will see Erg El Mhazil, Erg Lmhazil, Erg Elmhazil or Erg Lamhazil depending on the website or itinerary. Erg El Mhazil is the spelling local operators use most often.

Where are the El Mhazil dunes in Morocco?

The El Mhazil dunes lie in the desert around Foum Zguid in southern Morocco. You reach them by 4×4 from Foum Zguid, through Iriki National Park and across the Lake Iriki plain. There is no tarmac road to the dunes, so the trip is made with a local driver who knows the current state of the tracks.

Erg El Mhazil or Erg Chigaga: which should I choose?

Erg Chigaga is Morocco’s largest erg, with dunes mostly around 60–80 m, and the choice for big desert scenery. Erg El Mhazil is smaller and much quieter, ideal for a peaceful night or as the start of a trek. Both are reached from Foum Zguid by 4×4 across Lake Iriki, and our 7-day trek from Agadir takes in both.

How much does a night at Erg El Mhazil cost?

From Foum Zguid, our 2-day / 1-night trips start from €130 per person for the version with fossils, a village and an oasis, and from €140 for the Erg El Mhazil desert adventure. The 3-day desert hike with a nomad school visit starts from €260. Prices are confirmed with you before you book.

Can I visit Erg El Mhazil as a day trip?

Our Erg El Mhazil trips last at least two days with a night at camp, because evening and early morning are the best times in the dunes. If you only have a day, the Erg Chigaga day trip from Foum Zguid (from €125) or the Lake Iriki day trip are good alternatives. Ask us what fits your schedule.

Do I need to be fit to trek from Erg El Mhazil?

You don’t need to be an athlete. On our treks a camel caravan carries the kit, and the stages in our itineraries range from about two to four hours’ walking a day, some described as easy-paced. Reasonable general fitness is enough, but walking on sand is tiring, so ask for the stage details before you choose.

When is the best time to visit Erg El Mhazil?

From October to April, when daytime temperatures are comfortable for walking. In winter, nights can fall close to freezing, so pack warm layers. Sandstorms can occur in spring. Summer, with temperatures often above 40 °C, is not the time for trekking and is best kept for short trips.
