Ouarzazate to Erg Chigaga: a loop through the Draa and Iriki

The Ouarzazate to Erg Chigaga circuit via Foum Zguid (from €300) is a true loop: you reach the dunes from the east and return from the west without repeating a single stretch of road.

  • Day 1: 4×4 pick-up at your hotel at 8 am. The road follows the Draa Valley, with stops in Zagora and Tamegroute and lunch in M’Hamid. Beyond the Sacred Oasis you reach the dunes for sunset, dinner by the fire and a night at camp.
  • Day 2: sunrise and breakfast, then across Lake Iriki to Foum Zguid and the Zguid valley. Lunch in Tazenakht, back in Ouarzazate by late afternoon.

Foum Zguid lies about 150 km from Ouarzazate via Tazenakht; our Foum Zguid travel guide explains where it fits.

Zagora to Erg Chigaga and Foum Zguid in two days

Already in Zagora? The Zagora – Erg Chigaga – Foum Zguid circuit (from €235) extends a Draa Valley visit all the way to the big dunes.

  • Day 1: leave Zagora by 4×4 down the Draa Valley to M’Hamid, where an hour’s camel ride is included. The track continues to the Erg Chigaga camp for dinner, an evening by the fire and a night in a tent.
  • Day 2: sunrise and breakfast, then a 4×4 crossing to Lake Iriki and Foum Zguid, with the return to Zagora planned for around 1 pm.

Erg Chigaga is Morocco’s largest erg, around 35 to 40 km long; our Erg Chigaga guide covers what to expect.

Agadir to Erg Chigaga via Taroudant

From the coast, the Agadir to Erg Chigaga circuit via Taroudant (3 days / 2 nights, from €420) crosses the Anti-Atlas to the big dunes, with a night in Foum Zguid before the track.

  • Day 1: departure around 8:00 from Agadir, Taghazout or Tamraght. The walls of Taroudant, lunch on the way, a saffron stop in Taliouine, Foum Zguid by late afternoon and a guesthouse night.
  • Day 2: 4×4 track across Lake Iriki, coloured rocks and a fossil plateau, nomad-style lunch, the Erg Chigaga dunes for sunset, a camel ride and a night at camp.
  • Day 3: sunrise on the ridges, out of the desert and back to your hotel in the early evening.

The price is per person, based on 2 people, in a private 4×4 for up to 6.

Marrakech to Erg Chigaga: three or four days by 4×4, or on foot

By 4×4, the 3-day Marrakech to Erg Chigaga circuit (from €450) takes the direct road over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, past Aït Ben Haddou, Tazenakht and Foum Zguid, with a guesthouse night and then a night at camp, and returns through the Drâa valley. The 4-day version (from €570) adds a full day in the dunes and a second night at camp.

On foot, our circuits from Marrakech combine the transfer with several days of walking, while a camel caravan carries the gear.

Trek in the heart of the dunes, 5 days / 4 nights, from €650 (from Marrakech or Agadir):

  1. Leave Marrakech over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, or Agadir along the foot of the Atlas; on through Tazenakht, Foum Zguid and the Iriki plains to camp at Erg El Mhazil.
  2. Walk with the caravan to Erg Elhaj Ahmed and camp in the dunes.
  3. Another day’s walking to Erg El Ghoule, with an evening round the fire.
  4. Walk to El Grisim oasis for sand-baked bread, the tea ritual and a night under the palms.
  5. 4×4 back to Foum Zguid across the Iriki plain, a break in Tazenakht and the transfer to Marrakech or Agadir.

Oasis to Erg Chigaga trek, 6 days / 5 nights, from €650: transfer from Agadir or Marrakech and a night in a riad in Foum Zguid, 4×4 to El Grisim oasis, about three hours’ walk to Erg Chigaga, a day to a remote dune area, Erg El Ghoule, then back to Foum Zguid at around 11 am for the transfer.

Seven days from Agadir to Erg Chigaga: the crossing on foot

Agadir to Chigaga desert trekking (7 days / 6 nights, from €750) is our longest circuit.

  1. Agadir, Taroudant and Taliouine, into Iriki National Park and camp at Erg El Mhazil.
  2. Walk with the camel caravan to Erg Elhaj Ahmed.
  3. On to Erg El Ghoule, watching for desert wildlife and plants; an evening of stories and music.
  4. El Grisim oasis: the role of oases, sand-baked bread, tea and a night under the palms.
  5. Semi-arid tamarisk country and camp at the foot of the dunes.
  6. The final walk to Erg Chigaga and the climb up the great dune for sunset.

The published programme sets out these six stages; arrangements for the return on day seven are confirmed with your quote. For difficulty, walking hours and kit, see our Morocco desert trek guide.

Fitting a desert circuit into your Morocco itinerary

The desert slots easily into a wider trip, as long as you think about logistics:

  • Flying into Marrakech: crossing the High Atlas is part of the journey. It is easier to spend the night before and the night after in Marrakech.
  • Staying in Agadir: the three-day circuit adds the desert to a beach holiday; the seven-day trek suits travellers who want the Sahara to be the trip.
  • Ouarzazate and the Draa Valley: if you pass through Ouarzazate or Zagora, our circuits avoid driving the same road twice.
  • Flights: avoid an evening flight on the day you return; desert timings are approximate.
  • Season: October to April is best; in summer, days are often above 40 °C.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need for a multi-day Morocco desert tour?

Two days are enough for a night at Erg Chigaga from Zagora, Ouarzazate or Agadir, although they are full days on the road. To walk in the dunes and sleep at several camps, allow five to seven days: our walking circuits last five, six or seven days, from €650.

Can I do a circuit from Marrakech to Erg Chigaga?

Yes. By 4×4, the Marrakech to Erg Chigaga circuit via Foum Zguid takes 3 days (from €450) or 4 days (from €570), with the Tizi n’Tichka pass, Aït Ben Haddou and a night at camp. On foot, the Trek in the heart of the dunes (5 days, from €650) and the Oasis to Erg Chigaga trek (6 days, from €650) also include the transfer from Marrakech.

How much is the Agadir to Erg Chigaga circuit?

The Agadir to Erg Chigaga circuit via Taroudant takes 3 days / 2 nights and starts from €420 per person, based on 2 people. It includes a guesthouse night in Foum Zguid, the 4×4 crossing of Lake Iriki and a night at camp at Erg Chigaga. The price is confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

What is the difference between a circuit and a desert excursion?

On our site, an excursion starts in Foum Zguid and lasts one to three days. A circuit starts in a city such as Agadir, Marrakech, Ouarzazate or Zagora and includes the journey to the desert. If you are already in Foum Zguid, an excursion is simpler and involves much less driving.

Are the circuits private?

Yes, every circuit is a private departure, with the vehicle and guide reserved for your group. The published programme is the starting point; a different departure city or an extra night can be considered on request, with the price confirmed before you book.

When is the best time for a desert circuit in Morocco?

Roughly from October to April. In summer, days are often above 40 °C, which makes walking circuits inadvisable. In winter, desert nights can drop close to freezing, so bring warm clothes for the camp. Spring can bring wind and sandstorms.

Does the Ouarzazate circuit come back the same way?

No. It leaves Ouarzazate through the Draa Valley, Zagora, Tamegroute and M’Hamid, then returns via Lake Iriki, Foum Zguid and Tazenakht. It is a two-day, one-night loop from €300 per person, with a night at camp in Erg Chigaga and no stretch of road driven twice.
